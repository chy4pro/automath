Title: Crown-free families and forbidden subposets with $e(P)\in \{1,2\}$
Authors: Balázs Patkós, Casey Tompkins
Primary arXiv category: math.CO
Abstract: The maximum size of a weak $P$-free family $\mathcal{F}\subseteq 2^{[n]}$ is denoted by $La(n,P)$. Let $e(P)$ denote the maximum integer $k$ such that the union of any $k$ consecutive layers of $2^{[n]}$ is weak $P$-free. In recent years, multiple examples of posets with $e(P)<π^-(P):=\liminf_{n\to\infty} \frac{La(n,P)}{\binom{n}{\lfloor \frac{n}{2}\rfloor}}$ have been found.
