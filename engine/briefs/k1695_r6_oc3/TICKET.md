# K6-OC3 — the single opposite-chart lemma (OC_n) at n = 3 and tests at n = 4 (screen: sol; light)
Background (registry §R6.63, D1): B is cyclic iff B is conjugate into the unreduced upper-Hessenberg cell (H_{ij} = 0 for i > j+1, H_{j+1,j} != 0). Conjecture (OC_n): for every A in GL(n, F-bar) there are a permutation P and a lower unitriangular u with u^{-1} A P u unreduced upper Hessenberg. (OC_4) holds over GF(2) (verified: the union of u H u^{-1} P^{-1} is all of GL(4,2)). (OC_n) implies 16.95.
Task: (1) PROVE (OC_3) for every field (3 flag variables in u, one equation H_{31} = 0 and two pivots H_{21}, H_{32} != 0; six permutations; the statement is that for every invertible 3x3 A some P admits such a u) — or find a counterexample over some small field with an exhaustive verifier. (2) Test (OC_4) exhaustively over GF(3) and GF(4) with a recursive flag solver (choose u column by column: u_1 = e_1 + lower part, require A P u_1 in span(u_1, u_2) etc.), reporting any A with no (P, u). (3) If (OC_3) is proved, write the proof so that it could be formalised (explicit polynomial identities). Deliver: proof text (markdown, statuses PROVED/COMPUTED/FAILED per claim), scripts, logs, DONE-oc3.
