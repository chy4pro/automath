# Structure of ONE-GENERATED finite magmas satisfying equation 677. Do not search the internet.

You are given a finite magma (M, *) satisfying, for all x, y:
   (E677)   x = y * (x * ((y * x) * y)).
Facts you may use (all proved): every left translation L_y : z ↦ y*z is a bijection (write
y\z for its inverse); (KEY) (y*x)*y = x\(y\x); (E255) ((x*x)*x)*x = x is an OPEN question
in general but you may ASSUME it as an extra hypothesis in Part B. Known finite examples are
all AFFINE: x*y = αx + βy + c over a finite abelian group (e.g. x*y = 4x+3y and 4x+y over
Z/7; x*y = x + Gy over (Z/3)² with G = [[0,1],[1,1]]; x*y = 5x − 4y + 1 over Z/31, which
has no idempotent), and extensions of affine bases by fibres.
Definitions: for x ∈ M let ⟨x⟩ be the submagma generated by x (all terms in x). Put
S(x) = x*x, U(x) = (x*x)*x, H(x) = S(x)*U(x) = (xx)((xx)x).

a, b ∈ ⟨x⟩. (Data: this holds in every known model, with 0 failures over ~50,000 pairs; but
H is NOT a global homomorphism in some models — H(x*y) ≠ H(x)*H(y) happens for x, y

PART B. Prove or refute: every finite monogenic (one-generated) E677 magma is affine, i.e.
isomorphic to (A, x*y = αx + βy + c) for a finite abelian group A and endomorphisms α, β.
(In all known examples the monogenic submagmas are ≅ Z/7 with 4x+y or 4x+3y, ≅ (Z/3)² with
x+Gy, or ≅ Z/31 with 5x−4y+1.) Hints: in a monogenic magma generated by x, consider the

PART C (if A or B resists). Under the extra hypothesis E255 and "no idempotent" (x*x ≠ x for
