# K6-R2CERT — the rank-two ideal for Kourovka 16.95 at n = 4: one Gröbner basis per characteristic (screen: sol; ONE heavy process at a time, 2.5 GB cap)
Idea (registry §R6.70 §8 + §R6.42): if every A P_sigma is non-cyclic for some A in GL(4,F), then after scaling A = I + R with rank R <= 2 (A itself non-cyclic gives an eigenvalue lambda with rank(A - lambda I) <= 2). Write R = U W^T with U, W 4x2 symbolic (16 variables u_{i1},u_{i2},w_{j1},w_{j2}); then A P_sigma = P_sigma + U (P_sigma^T W)^T. A P_sigma is non-cyclic iff for some t in the algebraic closure rank(A P_sigma - t I) <= 2 iff all sixteen 3x3 minors of (A P_sigma - t I) vanish. IMPORTANT: unlike the rank-one case, t need NOT be an eigenvalue of P_sigma (a rank-two update can lower the rank of the invertible P_sigma - tI from 4 to 2), so use a FREE variable t_sigma per permutation with no chi constraint; the minors alone express rank <= 2. The identity permutation is bad automatically (rank(A - I) <= 2). So define, for each of the 23 non-identity sigma, a variable t_sigma and the 16 minors of (A P_sigma - t_sigma I); add c*z - 1 with c = det(A) (as a polynomial in U, W; det(I + UW^T) = det(I_2 + W^T U)). The ideal J_p in GF(p)[U, W, z, t_sigma] is the UNIT ideal iff no A of this form has all 24 permutations bad over the algebraic closure of GF(p) — i.e. iff Kourovka 16.95 holds for n = 4 over every field of characteristic p.
