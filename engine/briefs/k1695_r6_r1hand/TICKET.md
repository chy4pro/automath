# K6-R1HAND — hand proof of Kourovka 16.95 on the RANK-ONE stratum at n = 4 over every field (screen: sol; 0 heavy; light sympy under run_capped 2.5 GB)
Why: the rank-one stratum (A = I + uwᵀ, 1 + wᵀu ≠ 0) is certified by Gröbner bases in characteristic 0 and every prime < 10⁴ (registry §R6.42, §R6.78), but "every field" needs either the ℤ-content of those certificates (a 19-hour Singular lift is still running) or a HAND proof. A hand proof is plausible because rank-one updates can only lower rank by one: AP_σ = P_σ + u w_σᵀ is non-cyclic iff for some EIGENVALUE r of P_σ (a root of unity of order dividing a cycle length) rank(P_σ − rI + u w_σᵀ) ≤ 2, and by Lemma T (registry §R6.3/§R6.70): for corank-1 M = P_σ − rI (r a simple eigenvalue) this is qᵀu = 0, w_σᵀp = 0, 1 + w_σᵀM⁺u = 0 (p, q right/left kernel vectors); for corank-2 M it is u ∈ col M or w_σ ∈ row M. No non-eigenvalue case exists. So "σ bad" is a finite disjunction of explicit linear-plus-one-scalar conditions on (u, w) indexed by the eigenvalues of P_σ.
Task: prove that for every field F and all u, w ∈ F⁴ with 1 + wᵀu ≠ 0 some AP_σ is cyclic. Suggested route: show that the six 4-cycles cannot all be bad unless u and w satisfy strong linear relations (Fourier coefficients along each cycle), then handle those special (u, w) with 3-cycles/double transpositions/transpositions; treat characteristic 2 (only eigenvalue 1 for 2- and 4-cycles; ω for 3-cycles) and characteristic 3 (ω = 1) separately where the eigenvalue sets degenerate. Data you may use: exhaustive g-histograms and minimizers over GF(2), GF(3), GF(4) (registry §R6.69); the certificate results; the n = 4 rank-one all-fail examples do NOT exist (the theorem is true; you are proving it). Mark PROVED / FAILED per step; deliver PROOF.md with a complete case tree, sanity scripts, DONE-r1hand. Stretch goal (separate section, CONJECTURED/PROVED): the same for all n — for A = I + uwᵀ ∈ GL(n,F) some AP_σ is cyclic (the rank-one stratum for every n would be a new general result).
