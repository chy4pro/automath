The specific claim under test (the "least i with v_i ≠ 0" rule): let i be an index with v_i ≠ 0;
scale u ↦ v_i u, v ↦ v/v_i (A unchanged) so v_i = 1; delete row i of A to get the 3×4 matrix

y = (v_j)_{j≠i}. (Deflation: e_i is Krylov-cyclic for A P_σ with σ(i) = j and σ(≠i) = τ iff
b = R[:, j] is a cyclic vector of M = R[:, τ] (3×3), i.e. det[b, Mb, M²b] ≠ 0.) Claim: for every
(x, y) ∈ F³ × F³ with x ≠ 0 [and for every z := v_i·u_i with 1 + z + xᵀy ≠ 0 — check whether this
invertibility condition is even needed], some (j, τ) has det[b, Mb, M²b] ≠ 0.
