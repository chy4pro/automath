1. `krylov_det_feedback` (Lemma "Feedback invariance"): for M : Matrix (Fin m) (Fin m) K, b : Fin m → K, ℓ : Fin m → K, det (krylovMatrix (M + colVec b ⬝ rowVec ℓ) b) = det (krylovMatrix M b), where krylovMatrix T b has columns b, T b, …, T^{m−1} b.
