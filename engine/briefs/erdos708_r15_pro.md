# ATTACK — Erdős Problem #708, round 15: TRANSPORT (position-dependent) certificates for the sparse core (TEMPLATE v2.3, prove-or-refute)

## The statement (unchanged; the last gap to a linear bound for all n)
Atoms p^j ≤ m/64 with weights α_{p,j} ∈ [0,1], Σ_j α_{p,j} ≤ 1; S₀(n) = Σ α_{p,j} 1[p^j | n]; m > 4096; H = Σ α_{p,j}/p^j < 17/16.
K = {1,…,m}, I = {x+1,…,x+m}; L := Σ_{k≤m}(S₀(k) − 64)⁺, R := Σ_{b∈I}(S₀(b) − 1)⁺.   (SC_64): L ≤ R for every x ≥ 0.
PROVED (refereed): (SC_64) for 4096 < m ≤ 10^2942 (arithmetic shadows); moment bound L ≤ mH^65/65! < 6.24·10^−90 m; single high point ⇒ R > 63.

## Two barriers now known (both refereed or under referee; do NOT re-derive)
B1 (round 12/13): certificates c_D ≥ 0 valid for ALL windows (Σ_{D|b} c_D ≤ (S₀(b)−1)⁺ for every b ∈ ℕ) cannot reach L beyond m ≈ 10^2958.
B2 (round 14): window-adapted certificates (one coefficient c_D per modulus, feasibility only on I) fail on reflected windows I = (m! − m, m!] with P = n primes, α_{p,1} = 1: the point B = m! is divisible by every D ≤ m and has capacity n − 1, so Σ_D c_D ≤ n − 1, while cold reflected points B − D (ω_P(D) ≤ 1) force c_D = 0 for low-rank D; hence W(I) ≤ (n−1)⌊m/(p₁p₂)⌋ ≪ C(n,65) ≤ L_64. Yet on these windows (SC_64) holds with room: S₀(B − t) = S₀(t) gives R ≥ L + 63.
Diagnosis: a single coefficient per modulus D must "pay" at every multiple of D in I, including the LCM-rich hot point; on B2 windows the mass of L (at 65-fold products k_A ≤ m) is matched one-to-one by the reflected points B − k_A (capacity 64 each), but the certificate cannot express "use the multiple B − k_A of k_A, not the multiple B".

## The transport certificate (the object of this round)
Variables: a K-side covering c'_D ≥ 0 (D ≤ m) with Σ_{D|k} c'_D ≥ (S₀(k) − 64)⁺ for all k ≤ m (so L ≤ Σ_D c'_D ⌊m/D⌋ =: Λ(c')), and an I-side placement c_{D,b} ≥ 0 for b ∈ I, D | b, D ≤ m, with
 (cap) Σ_{D|b} c_{D,b} ≤ (S₀(b) − 1)⁺ for every b ∈ I, and (route) Σ_{b∈I, D|b} c_{D,b} ≥ c'_D ⌊m/D⌋ for every D.
Then R ≥ Σ_b Σ_{D|b} c_{D,b} ≥ Σ_D c'_D⌊m/D⌋ ≥ L. Define T(I) := max Λ(c') over feasible (c', c). T(I) ≥ W(I) always. On B2 windows T(I) ≥ L (take c'_{k_A} = 1 for every 65-subset A, c_{k_A, B−k_A} = 1). Equivalent flow form: source mass (S₀(k)−64)⁺ at k ∈ K must be routed through "modulus channels" D | k to points b ∈ I with D | b, respecting capacities (S₀(b)−1)⁺; a modulus channel D carries mass c'_D from each of its ⌊m/D⌋ multiples in K to its multiples in I.
Dual (write it out; it is a two-sided potential problem): u_k ≥ 0 on K-hot points, v_b ≥ 0 on I, with u_k ≤ … — derive the exact dual and use it to build barriers.

## Targets (prove or refute; every claim with a checkable certificate; the referee re-runs everything)
T1 (closure attempt): prove T(I) ≥ L for every window in the sparse regime (H < 17/16, m > 4096). Suggested structure: (a) for each K-hot k (S₀(k) > 64) pick a modulus D_k | k that carries most of k's atom mass (D_k = product of the top prime powers active at k; by the moment bound there are ≤ 6.24·10^−90 m hot k); (b) show the multiples of D_k in I carry enough spare capacity: a multiple b of D_k has S₀(b) ≥ (mass of D_k's atoms) > 64 − (leftover), so (S₀(b)−1)⁺ ≥ 63 − …; (c) the only obstruction is CROWDING: many distinct hot k routing to the same b. Bound the crowding: b ∈ I receives from k only if D_k | b; the number of hot k with D_k | b is at most the number of hot divisors-patterns of b — relate it to S₀(b) (a point with S₀(b) = s has at most C(s-ish, 65) hot patterns, but its capacity is only s − 1 — this is the crux; compare with the moment bound on K-side hot points and with how many multiples of D_k lie in I (⌊m/D_k⌋ or +1)). If T1 fails, say exactly where.
T2 (barrier attempt): construct a window (explicit family, all hypotheses verified) with T(I) < L. Use the dual: a potential that is cheap on I yet covers every hot k. Candidates: windows around numbers B with many but not all D ≤ m dividing B (e.g. B = lcm of a chosen set), congruence-defined windows (round 12/13 families), windows where every multiple of every hot-pattern D in I is hot (so capacities are shared by many patterns) — "hot-pattern crowding" windows.
T3 (finite exact experiments, exact arithmetic): for small analogues (threshold C ∈ {2,3} instead of 64, m ≤ 3000, few primes) compute T(I) by LP for adversarial windows (m!-windows, lcm-windows, congruence windows) and compare with L_C and R; report the minimal ratio T/L found and any T < L instance. Give the code.
T4 (consequence): if T1 closes, state the resulting theorem exactly: (SC_64) for all m > 4096 ⇒ (TH_65) ⇒ g(n) ≤ 81n for all n. If T2 closes, state what remains (which cone could still work, or whether (SC_64) is likely false).

## Referee addendum to round 14 (use this first; it narrows the question)
The whole-window feasibility requirement is what dies, not the cone. Define the HOT-SET-EXCLUDED window LP: choose T ⊆ I (the hot points; |T| ≤ 0.015m by the first-moment bound, and |T| = 1 on the B2 family), require Σ_{D|b} c_D ≤ (S₀(b) − 1)⁺ only for b ∈ I ∖ T, and value W_T(I) := Σ_D c_D · #{b ∈ I ∖ T : D | b}. Then R ≥ Σ_{b∈I∖T} Σ_{D|b} c_D = W_T(I), so W_T(I) ≥ L for some T proves (SC_64) on I. On the B2 family with T = {B}: c_D = 64 on all 65-prime products gives W_T = 64·L_64; on the C = 2 witness c_{pq} = 1/3 gives 13.8·L_2. So:
T1' (primary closure attempt): prove sup_T W_T(I) ≥ L for every sparse window (H < 17/16, m > 4096). Natural T = {b ∈ I : S₀(b) > 72} (then |T| < 0.015m and every b ∉ T has S₀(b) ≤ 72). For a K-hot k with pattern D_k (product of its top active prime powers, S₀-mass > 64 − ε), the multiples of D_k in I ∖ T all have S₀ ≥ 64 − ε and capacity ≥ 63 − ε; crowding: a point b ∉ T with S₀(b) ≤ 72 can be a multiple of at most (number of 65-mass sub-patterns of b) patterns — bound this by the structure of b's atom multiset (S₀(b) ≤ 72 leaves at most ~8 units of slack; count sub-patterns of mass > 64 among atoms summing to ≤ 72). Route each k's unit mass to its ⌊m/D_k⌋ − |T| ≥ … multiples; if ⌊m/D_k⌋ = 1 (D_k > m/2) handle separately (such k are few: their D_k > m/2 are distinct integers dividing distinct k's…). Write out the full argument or the exact failure point.
T2' (barrier attempt): a family with |T| LARGE and W_{I∖T} < L for every admissible T — e.g. windows where the hot points are numerous and every multiple of every hot pattern in I is hot (crowding) — or prove no such family exists in the sparse regime.
The transport certificate above remains the general fallback (T1/T2); T3/T4 unchanged. Always state which cone each result is about.

## Coordinator notes for T1' (own analysis, 09-05; use or refute)
(i) Natural certificate: for each K-hot k let D_k be its full active pattern (product of the active prime powers), so S₀(D_k) = S₀(k) and every multiple b of D_k has S₀(b) ≥ S₀(k). Aggregate c_D = Σ_{k : D_k = D}(S₀(k) − 64)/N_{I∖T}(D). Its value is exactly L (global identity), so only feasibility on I ∖ T matters.
(ii) Crowding: at b ∉ T, Σ_{hot D | b} c_D counts the hot patterns dividing b. Patterns with many multiples (D ≤ m/9) contribute ≤ 8·(m/D)/(⌊m/D⌋ − t_D) ≈ 8 per pattern only if b is a common multiple of several hot patterns, which forces those patterns to overlap in > 56 units of mass. Patterns with D > m/9 have ≤ 8 multiples in I.
(iii) T cannot be "S₀ > 72": a k with S₀(k) = 100 has all its multiples in that T (N_{I∖T}(D_k) = 0). T must be chosen as the set of points where the natural certificate is infeasible (LCM-rich common multiples), and the loss must be compensated globally (the principle is Σ_D c_D N_{I∖T}(D) ≥ L in total, not per k). |T| ≤ 0.015m by the first moment, but hot patterns have few multiples, so T can swallow all multiples of some patterns — bound the swallowed mass.
(iv) Local exact LPs (problems/erdos708/hotset_lp.py) at toy scale (C = 2, 3; primes ≤ 13; m ≤ 100) are uninformative (L ≤ 1); the barrier scale needs m ≈ p³ with ~p/ln p primes in [p, 2p], p ≳ 300, beyond exact LP — argue analytically.
