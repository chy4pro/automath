A new explicit bound for the second-order term, together with a limitation result for the method.

(1) For every Sidon set A in {1,...,N} with N >= 120^4: |A| <= sqrt(N) + (2 sqrt(2)/3) N^{1/4} + 1, where 2 sqrt(2)/3 = 0.942809... The proof is the weighted Erdős–Turán energy argument with the kernel h(t) = 2(1-t) on [0,1] (diagonal cost f(0) = 4/3), plus an exact boundary term: for this kernel the capacity of an interval of length L is L + 2/3 up to an exponentially small error, in place of the L + 1 that gives Lindström's bound.

(2) Within this method the constant is optimal: for every even nonnegative kernel f with integral 1, liminf (C_f(L) - L) >= 8/(9 f(0)), with equality for the ramp, so the fixed-kernel capacity argument cannot give a coefficient below sqrt(8/9) = 2 sqrt(2)/3 for any choice of scale. This appears to be the limit that the recent numerical values (0.94349, 0.94324, 0.94301) have been approaching.
