**The exact printed statement and the proposed correction’s scope**

READ — P. Erdős, R. L. Graham, I. Z. Ruzsa and E. G. Straus, *On the prime factors of* \(\binom{2n}{n}\), **Mathematics of Computation 29 (129) (1975), 83–92**, [DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-1975-0369288-3](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1975-0369288-3). **Theorem 4, printed p. 89**, was inspected in the [primary scan](https://renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1975-27.pdf#page=7), including its divisibility symbol. With \(B_n=\binom{2n}{n}\), it prints, for \(\alpha<1\),

\[
\#\{1\le m\le n^\alpha:m\nmid B_n\}
 =c(\alpha)n^\alpha+o(n^\alpha),\qquad
c(\alpha)\longrightarrow1\quad(\alpha\longrightarrow0).
\]

READ — The theorem contains no almost-all qualifier. The paragraph immediately before it explicitly uses “for almost all n” for the localized reciprocal-prime assertion (6), then invokes the sieve method. The theorem adds that the constant “can be explicitly calculated”. No explicit formula, numerical table, or detailed sieve derivation is supplied there. The exact omission relevant to this audit is this transition from the averaged/localized statement to the sieve-density assertion; this report does not repair or validate that transition.

READ / LIVE CLAIM — The internal manuscript [§§5–7 of `erdos377_r1_astra.md`](/work/engine/harvest/erdos377_r1_astra.md:187) states the correction only for **fixed \(0<\alpha\le1/2\)**. Its quantifier is: for every such α and every η > 0,

\[
\#\{n\le X:|N_\alpha(n)-c_N(\alpha)|>\eta\}=o(X),
\qquad N_\alpha(n)=n^{-\alpha}\#\{m\le n^\alpha:m\nmid B_n\},
\]

with the analogous assertion for divisors and \(c_D=1-c_N\). It claims \(c_N(\alpha)\to0\), \(c_D(\alpha)\to1\), and no universal all-n limit in this range. Its cited obstruction is \(v_2(B_{2^h})=1\), combined with its density result. These are **internal claims under the separate referee/verifier gate**, not conclusions independently proved by Scout. Do not silently extend the correction to every \(0<\alpha<1\), or present the obstruction alone as establishing nonexistence for that entire range.

READ / LIVE CLAIM — The manuscript’s formula, recorded here to identify precisely what was searched for, is

\[
w(t)=2^{-\lfloor1/t\rfloor},\quad J_0=1,\quad
J_k(\alpha)=\frac1{k!}\int_{t_i>0,\ \sum t_i\le\alpha}
 \prod_{i=1}^k\frac{w(t_i)}{t_i}\,dt_1\cdots dt_k,
\quad c_D=\sum_{k\ge0}(-1)^kJ_k,\quad c_N=1-c_D.
\]

READ / LIVE CLAIM — For \(s=\lfloor1/\alpha\rfloor\), \(A_s=2^{1-s}/s\), it gives the absolute tail bound
\[
\left|c_D-\sum_{k=0}^{K}(-1)^kJ_k\right|
\le\frac{A_s^{K+1}}{(K+1)!\,[1-A_s/(K+2)]},\qquad K\ge0.
\]
Its effective constant evaluation does **not** supply an effective threshold in n for the exceptional-set asymptotic. The manuscript explicitly distinguishes these two meanings of effectiveness.
