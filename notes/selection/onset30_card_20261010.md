\[
 \forall N\in\mathbb Z,\quad N\ge207\,360\,000,\quad
 \forall A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}\text{ strong Sidon},\qquad

The onset is \(N_0=120^4=207\,360\,000\) (READ; equality also TRIVIAL ARITHMETIC).
