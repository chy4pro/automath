## Steps re-derived and how

**(a) §5/§5.1, the density limit.**
* Decomposition. m ∤ B_n holds iff some p has v_p(m) > v_p(B_n). Either p ∤ B_n and p | m, or v_p(B_n) >= 1 and p^{v_p+1} | m. So 1 - Phi_n - 1/x <= N_alpha(n) <= 1 - Phi_n + T(n), where Phi_n is the fraction of m <= x = n^alpha with no prime factor in P_n = {p <= x : p ∤ B_n}. Splitting P_n at n^delta changes Phi by at most G_delta(n). Checked.
* Sieve. Every d in the inclusion–exclusion is squarefree and built from P_n ∩ (n^delta, n^alpha], so it has at most floor(alpha/delta) prime factors. The error from replacing floor(x/d)/x by 1/d is bounded in two parts:
  - d <= n^{alpha-eps}: at most n^{-eps} prod(1+1/p) <= n^{-eps} e^{mu_n}.
  - The slab alpha-eps < sum t_i <= alpha: each term is off by at most 1/d, so the error is at most the slab mass.

  The diagonal (repeated-prime) mass is at most k^2 (sum_{p>n^delta} p^-2) mu_n^{k-2} -> 0. Checked.
* Where independence across primes enters. The sieve is deterministic for each n. The only "random" input is the measure mu_n, and the k-fold sums are (up to the diagonal) mu_n^{⊗k}(simplex)/k!. So no joint-independence assumption on the events {p ∤ B_n} is made beyond what is in (LOCAL). (LOCAL)'s variance step is where pairwise near-independence is needed. EGRS prove it by scale separation (condition (5)), not by assuming it. The point is that the condition "k mod w_j < w_j/2" with w_{j+1}/w_j > x^eps nests into unions of intervals, which gives x/2^{r+t}(1+O((r+t)x^{-eps})) with no coprimality or CRT needed. Kummer's criterion (p ∤ B_k iff k mod p^i < p^i/2 for all i) is the exact form of the carry condition used. Checked.
* Convergence of mu_n^{⊗k}(simplex) in density. This uses finitely many bins, the inner and outer box sums of products of mu_n(bin), and (LOCAL) on each bin. Finitely many density-zero exceptional sets have a density-zero union. The outer-minus-inner gap is O(h) under the absolutely continuous limit. Checked.
* T(n). The tail sum over p > H is at most 1/(H-1). For fixed p, I checked the block-counting bound 2·P(Bin(h, floor(p/2)/p) <= V). This includes p=2 (a=b=1) and the partial-block argument. Checked.
* G_delta. Markov gives density({G_delta > eta}) <= A(delta)/eta (see R3). Then let delta -> 0, and use J_k^{(delta)} -> J_k with dominated convergence (J_k <= A^k/k!). Checked.
* Quantifiers. The result is almost-all n (natural density) for each fixed alpha. No effective exceptional-set rate is claimed, and none is proved. The constant is effective.
* Mertens remark (line 266). This is correct with the Rosser–Schoenfeld lower bound valid for x > 1, upper bound for x >= 286. It is only used for boundedness.
