The congruence \(n^2 \equiv -1 \pmod{101}\) has solutions because \(101 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}\). The solutions modulo 101 are \(n \equiv 10\) and \(n \equiv 91\) (since \(10^2 = 100 \equiv -1\) and \(91 \equiv -10\)).

For \(1 \le n \le 1000\):
- \(n = 10 + 101k\): \(k = 0,1,\dots,9\) gives 10 values.
- \(n = 91 + 101k\): \(k = 0,1,\dots,9\) gives 10 values.

No overlap, so total = 20.

ANSWER: 20
