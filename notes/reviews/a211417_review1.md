## JUSTIFICATION GAP

### J1. The stated asymptotic bound for `D(r)` is false

- **Location:** lines 202--203.
- **Claim:** The displayed estimate for `E(p,r)` is followed by
  `log D(r)=O(r log log r)`.
- **Finding:** The displayed estimate gives at best

  ```text
  log D(r)
    <= r sum_{p<=r} log(p)/(p-1) + sum_{p<=r} log(r)
    = O(r log r),
  ```

  not `O(r log log r)`.  This is not merely a weak derivation of a possibly
  true result.  For every eligible prime `p`, the elements
  `1, 1+30p, 1+60p, ...` which do not exceed `r` all belong to `L_r` and lie
  in one residue class modulo `p`.  Consequently

  ```text
  E(p,r) >= floor((r-1)/(30p)) + 1.
  ```

  Summing `E(p,r) log p` over primes up to `(r-1)/30`, and using the standard
  estimate `sum_{p<=x} log(p)/p ~ log x`, gives
  `log D(r)=Omega(r log r)`.  Thus the stated `O(r log log r)` corollary is
  incompatible with the explicit `D(r)` defined in Theorem 3.
- **Impact:** A false auxiliary corollary.  It does not enter the divisibility
  proof.

### J2. The “complete classification” and “completely infeasible” direction is not proved

- **Location:** lines 227--230 and 251--253; repeated in the general
  height-one discussion at lines 289--290.  The verification script also
  labels the finite zero check as “correctly excluded” at lines 35--37.
- **Claim:** Feasibility of `qn +/- i` is said to be equivalent to the relevant
  step-function cells all being `1`; in particular, `30n+i` is declared
  completely impossible.
- **Finding:** The calculation proves the local statements

  ```text
  p^j | qn+i and p^j >= mi  ==>  Delta(n/p^j)=Delta_(mc-1),
  Delta_(c-1)=0 for the 30n+i unit cells.
  ```

  The all-ones condition is therefore sufficient for the high-layer argument.
  But a zero in these cells, by itself, is not a proof that no constant can
  make the corresponding divisibility family hold.  That necessity direction
  requires an additional construction of unbounded prime-power divisors which
  are not supplied by other Legendre layers and cannot be absorbed by a fixed
  constant.  No such argument is present.  The script checks only the cell
  values and likewise does not establish global impossibility.
- **Impact:** The overclaimed classification and the `Nn+i` impossibility
  conclusion are unsupported.  The positive `k=2,3,5` result in Theorem 4
  uses only the sufficient direction and is unaffected.

## Independent mathematical verification

### 1. Legendre reduction and the step function

Legendre's formula gives, term by term,

```text
v_p(a(n)) = sum_{j>=1} Delta(n/p^j).
```

The coefficient balance `30+1-15-10-6=0` proves period one.  On the cell
`[j/30,(j+1)/30)`, direct substitution gives

```text
Delta_j = j-floor(j/2)-floor(j/3)-floor(j/5).
```

Independent evaluation for `j=0,...,29` reproduces the displayed table and
only the values `0` and `1`.  The unit residues modulo 30 are

```text
1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29,
```

and direct evaluation of the above integer formula gives `Delta_j=1` at all
eight residues.  Thus (2.2) and Lemma A are correct independently of the
proof's interval-multiple argument.

### 2. Lemma B

Writing `s=n mod p^k` gives `30s=i+c p^k`.  The strict inequality `p^k>i`
places `c` in `0,...,29` and puts `s/p^k` in cell `c`.  Because `p` is a unit
modulo 30, reducing the equation modulo each of `2,3,5` shows that `c` is a
unit modulo 30; in particular, it is nonzero.  Lemma A then supplies exactly
the Legendre layer indexed by this same `k`.  This reasoning is unaffected
when `30n-i` is negative.

### 3. Lemma C and cross-power accounting

- If `p^k>r`, two witnesses imply `p^k | i-i'`, while
  `|i-i'|<=r-1<p^k`; hence the witness is unique.
- The divisibility sets are nested as `k` increases.  Therefore all nonempty
  high layers use one witness `i_0` and form an initial high-layer interval.
- The identity
  `v_p(P_r(n))=sum_k N_k` counts a factor of valuation `e` once on each of
  layers `1,...,e`.  Thus sharing the same prime across different powers is
  not a loss or a double charge: every high `k` is matched with the distinct
  term `Delta(n/p^k)=1` in `v_p(a(n))`.
- For `p^k<=r`, the actual `N_k` is bounded by the largest residue-class size
  modulo `p^k`.  Summing those bounds is exactly `E(p,r)` and is independent
  of `n`.

This establishes the asserted valuation inequality prime by prime.

### 4. Requested edge cases

- **Small `n`:** `30n-i` may be negative, but it is never zero because
  `i` is coprime to 30.  Integer divisibility and `p`-adic valuation use its
  absolute value, and the residue proof remains valid.
- **`n=0`:** no high power `p^k>r` divides an index `i<=r`; all valuations of
  `P_r(0)` are low-layer valuations covered by `E(p,r)`.
- **`i=r`:** the proof uses the strict inequality `p^K>r`, so Lemma B still
  has `p^K>i`; there is no equality leak.
- **`p in {2,3,5}`:** none divides any `30n-i` with `i in L_r`, so these
  primes contribute zero to `P_r(n)`.
- **Different powers of one prime:** handled layerwise as described above;
  the argument never incorrectly demands different witnesses for different
  powers.

### 5. The `C(k,r)` family

For `m=30/k`, reduction of `p^j | kn+i` gives

```text
Delta(n/p^j)=Delta_(mc-1)
```

once `p^j>=mi`.  Equality at the threshold is harmless: it lands exactly at
the left endpoint of cell `mc-1`.  For `k=2,3,5`, the seven required cells are
`14`; `9,19`; and `5,11,17,23`, all of which have value one.  Taking the
uniform high-layer threshold `p^K>mr` is stronger than necessary but valid.
Primes dividing `k` cannot divide `kn+i` because `(i,k)=1`; primes dividing
`m` need not be excluded.  The same high/low layer split therefore proves
Theorem 4.
