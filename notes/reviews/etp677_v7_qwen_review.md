## Full review, verbatim (harvested via `get_page_text`, numbering restored from the rendered page)

1. Location: Round 9, §sec:skeleton, Theorem thm:twolegs; Problem prob:Prop. Classification: CRITICAL ERROR. Justification: The theorem states (Prop) as varrho* ≠ ∇ and asserts this is equivalent to the existence of some pair a ≠ b with F_ab = ∅. Since varrho* is the transitive closure of the direct collapse relation, a connected collapse graph may still omit direct edges; existence of an empty direct F_ab does not imply varrho* is proper. Problem prob:Prop then uses the fixed-point-free-displacement reading and cites Theorem thm:twolegs, but (B) plus a fixed-point-free displacement does not establish a nontrivial congruence. The corrected skeleton therefore rests on an unproved and, as stated, false equivalence.

2. Location: Proposition prop:propauto; Problem prob:Prop. Classification: CRITICAL ERROR. Justification: The proposition proves automaticity of (Prop) only for pair-indexed extensions over a Latin base, then concludes unconditionally that (Prop) can fail only for a simple non-Latin magma. No argument is given that every relevant non-simple E677 magma must be an extension over a Latin base; the Latin-base reduction is available only under a minimal-counterexample hypothesis not stated here. A non-simple extension over a non-Latin base, or over a simple base where (Prop) fails, is not excluded. The unconditional claim and the assertion that no extension-based model can supply evidence are therefore unsupported.

3. Location: Round 9, Theorem thm:rigidity. Classification: CRITICAL ERROR. Justification: The bijectivity of (x,y) ↦ P4 is plausible, but the claimed unique determination of the P4 fibre operation from P1,P2,P3 is not established by the displayed formula. For diagonal pairs in the idempotent F_11 base used in M_77^D, one has P1 = P2 = P3 = P4, so the formula involves left divisions in the very operation it is supposed to determine. The statement "no single fibre operation may be perturbed in isolation" does not follow from the bijectivity of the P4 indexing and is not proved for self-referential diagonal cases.

4. Location: v7 changelog [B]; Round 9 Theorem thm:strata; retraction table in §sec:retraction. Classification: CRITICAL ERROR. Justification: The changelog states that the third-party order-385 object refutes (A), Filter 1, and Filter 2. The Round 9 stratum theorem instead says M_{385}^{canon} satisfies (A) and passes both filters exactly, and the retraction table uses precisely this fact to separate (A) from (T) and to expose the filter blind spot. These are directly incompatible descriptions of the same object. The retraction chain is internally inconsistent as to which results are refuted by which model.

5. Location: Lemma lem:dichotomy; retraction chain, "Restricted survivors"; VOID annotation on Theorem thm:dichotomy. Classification: JUSTIFICATION GAP. Justification: Lemma lem:dichotomy only characterizes clean fibre operations as Latin or constant-column. It does not prove that clean extensions satisfy (T), (W), (A), Filter 1, or Filter 2, nor that the voided dichotomy and marginal-count corollaries become valid on the clean class. The restricted-survivor paragraph nevertheless asserts that these laws and results hold for clean extensions. Without a separate proof, the restricted survival silently depends on the retracted hypothesis (T) or on an unproved implication.
