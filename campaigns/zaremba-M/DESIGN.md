### 3.1 E1-zaremba-explicit-M

**target_number:**

RECORD: Zaremba holds for all sufficiently large primes q=p with M = 2^2000. Source: Shkredov arXiv 2603.14116v2, Appendix eq. (158); I read the PDF text myself. q0 is not explicit, and for all q (Zhang 2605.02518) M is effective but never computed.

HOW THE NUMBER IS BUILT (read directly from the appendix and Sec. 5.4):
- M ~ max{10(kappa*eps)^-1, 100*delta^-60} as in (157), with eps < 1/18 from (156), M* >= 40M from Thm 38, plus the 4M of Lemma 10 and the 100M of (22).
- kappa = delta/6.
- delta = c/2^(k+4), with k = ceil(1/c)+1.
- c >= min{1/3, 1/(8C2), tau/(4C2), 0.5 c_H/(C1+C2)} = 1/1640, using C1=9, C2=32 (Murphy BSG, Thm 39) and c_H = 1/20 (Rudnev-Shkredov, Lemma 40).

My exact recomputation of the same chain gives log2 M ≈ 1/c + log2(1/c) + ~25 ≈ 1680, so 2^2000 is Shkredov's round-up. Two inconsistencies for referees: (a) the appendix states kappa >= 2^-1656, but kappa = delta/6 gives 2^-1658.3; (b) the appendix uses tau = 1/4, while MMS 2212.14646 has girth tau = 1/5 with m = (tau/4) log_N p. Recomputing 2^2000 to 2^~1680 is bookkeeping and must NOT be announced.

WHAT WE ANNOUNCE AS OUR NUMBER: the proved log2 M for all sufficiently large primes, stated as the same type of result (q0 not explicit).
- Minimal movement worth announcing: log2 M ≤ 500 (about c ≥ 1/450), obtained from a new lemma, not constant substitution, passing all 4 referees and G2.
- Strong success: log2 M ≤ 150 (c ≥ 1/110).
- Moonshot (R3/R4): kappa polynomial in the inputs instead of 2^(-1/c). With Shkredov's overheads kept, even kappa = 1/2 gives only M ~ 2^21 (my arithmetic). So a moonshot success lands at M ~ 2^20–2^40, and the overhead factors (40, 4, 100, 1/18) then become their own target (R5).

HEADROOM, measured by me: I computed sigma_max of the Lemma-14 operator T f(x) = (1/N) Σ_{j≤N} f(1/(x+2j) − 2j) on mean-zero functions on P^1(F_p). For p = 100003 and p = 1000003, N = 4..64, it equals 2*sqrt(N−1)/N to 3 decimals, which is the Akemann–Ostrand free-group (tempered) value. So the true kappa → 1/2, against the proven 2^-1658. Script and output: /work/notes/selection/zaremba_kappa_numerics.py and /work/notes/selection/zaremba_kappa_numerics.out. The whole gap is in the proof, not in the truth.
