TEXT FOR THE CLEAN-ROOM AGENTS:
Zaremba's conjecture: there is an absolute constant M such that every integer q ≥ 1 is the denominator of a reduced fraction a/q whose continued-fraction partial quotients are all ≤ M. Target: prove, for all sufficiently large PRIMES q, the existence of such a/q with all partial quotients ≤ M for an explicit M as small as you can (any explicit M is the goal; M ≤ 2^500 would be valuable, M ≤ 2^150 very valuable). You may use standard tools: continued fractions and SL2(Z) products of matrices [[a,1],[1,0]], the set of such products with bounded entries as a "thin" subset of SL2(Z), reduction mod q into SL2(F_q), counting/equidistribution in SL2(F_q), Fourier analysis on F_q, character sums, spectral gaps of random walks / expander arguments, sieve, or elementary counting. Give complete proofs of every step you claim; when an input is a known theorem, state it exactly with all constants explicit or mark the constant as unknown. If you cannot finish, deliver the most complete conditional theorem (clear hypothesis ⇒ explicit M) and the smallest missing lemma.
