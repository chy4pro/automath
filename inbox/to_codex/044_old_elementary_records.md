# 044 Selection scan: old, elementary, unoptimised records (status only; no attacks)
priority: high · parallel: yes · clean-room: no · report: /work/notes/selection/old_records_20261003.md (≤ 300 lines)

Reflection after today (coordinator): the hit (Sidon second order) came from a record whose proof was elementary and decades old with an unexploited constant (Lindström's boundary loss); the misses (B₃, B₂[g], hypercube C₄, distinct subset sums, and the 2√2/3 barrier) were all records that are saturated optimisations of a relaxation (LP/SDP/autoconvolution) where the remaining slack is structural. Your 042 pushback agrees. So the screen for round 3 is: **records whose best known bound is old (ideally pre-2005), proved by an elementary argument with an explicit constant or exponent, cited as "best known" by the problem page or a recent survey, and not the object of a recent computational optimisation.**

Part A (main): go through erdosproblems.com systematically (the open problems with a quantitative statement — use the site's tags/search and the OPEN list; also OEIS-linked ones) and list every problem where the quoted best bound meets the screen. For each: problem number; exact quantity and the quoted bound with source and year; the lossy step(s) in that proof (read the proof or a reliable survey statement; label READ/SECONDARY); whether a recent paper (2015–2026) touched it (quick search; if a recent improvement exists, drop the item); a one-line guess of which generic tool could tighten it (exact capacity/boundary accounting, exact marginals, energy + LP duality, finite certificates, better kernel, second moment → higher moments, probabilistic refinement) and the plausible size of the gain (heuristic, labelled). Aim for 15–30 items, ranked by (fame × plausibility × verifiability).

Part B (short): the same screen applied to records adjacent to our other machinery — the #708 LP-duality/hinge-certificate method (linear bounds for g(n) in Erdős–Surányi-type problems) and the #889 argument — are there neighbouring old bounds those tools improve mechanically, the way the capacity certificate did for sonar/DTS/weak Sidon? List up to 5 with the same fields.

Evidence over rank; say when the screen yields nothing in a region. No external posting.
