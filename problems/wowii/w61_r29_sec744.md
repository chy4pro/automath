### (d) **The finding that matters: Corollary C1-C's construction DOES NOT ALWAYS EXIST — Observation C1-E**

`problems/wowii/w61_r29_c1lead.py` → `.out`, `w61_r29_c1cfail.py` → `.out`.

Corollary C1-C says: **C1 holds for `λ` as soon as `M := [λ₁]^{λ₁+1} ∪ λ` has a
realization `G` with `α(G) ≤ k`.** That is a correct one-directional sufficiency and
the audit does not dispute it. The question nobody had asked is whether the graph it
asks for exists. It does not.

> **Observation C1-E.** Let `w ≥ 3` be **odd** and `λ = (w, 1)`. Then `|λ| = w+1` is
> even, `M = [w]^{w+2} ∪ [1]` **is** a step sequence, C1 holds for `λ` (Theorem C1-2:
> `s₀ = w+1 ≠ w`), and **every** realization of `M` has `α ≥ 3 > 2 = k`. Hence
> Corollary C1-C's hypothesis is **unsatisfiable** on an infinite family of genuine C1
> instances.
>
> *Proof.* `α(G) ≤ 2` iff the complement is triangle-free. `M` has `N = w+3` vertices,
> so the complement degrees are `(N−1) − w = 2` at each of the `w+2` entries of value
> `w`, and `(N−1) − 1 = w+1` at the single entry of value `1`. In a triangle-free
> complement the degree-`(w+1)` vertex `u` has `w+1` pairwise non-adjacent neighbours,
> each of complement-degree `2`, so each must spend its second edge at the **one**
> remaining vertex `z` — giving `deg(z) = w+1`, while `deg(z) = 2`. Contradiction for
> `w ≥ 2`. ∎

**Machine backing, both directions and two implementations.** A mask sweep over
**every labelled graph** on `≤ 7` vertices computes `minα(M) := min_G α(G)` exactly:
of the `13` rows with `k ≥ 2` and `|M| ≤ 7`, **`12` have `minα ≤ k` and one does
not** — `λ = 3+1`, `minα = 3 > 2 = k`, over all **`180`** realizations. A second,
structurally different enumerator (backtracking on forward neighbourhoods, early exit
on the first witness) reproduces **no realization with `α ≤ k`** at
`w = 3, 5, 7, 9`, and reproduces `α ≤ k` **YES** on all `7` control rows the mask
sweep called available. `residue ≤ minα` holds on every row (FMS, as imported), and
`residue ≤ k` holds on every row (C1, as conjectured).

**What this does and does not cost.** It costs **no theorem**: Corollary C1-C's
statement is a sufficiency and remains true; C1 is *true* at `λ = (w,1)` and proved
there by Theorem C1-2. It costs the **route**. And the way it was being carried is the
species this session keeps finding: **§7.25 (b) hedged correctly** — *"the construction
is not built here, and I claim nothing about whether it always exists"* — while
**§7.28 (d)2, the resume pointer, dropped the hedge** and filed C1 for `k ≥ 3` as
*"reduced by Corollary C1-C to exhibiting … one graph"*. **The source was right and the
layer that carries it to the next round was wrong. Fourth instance of that pattern in

### (e) **Item 2 — Conjecture C1 for `k ≥ 3`: what a first attack looks like, and whether it needs a seat**

Two things are now known that were not.

1. **The route named in the resume pointer is dead as a general method** (Observation
   C1-E). Any attack must either aim at a *different* invariant or accept that the
   `α ≤ k` construction covers only part of the range.
2. **The `α ≤ k` construction is available exactly when a `k`-clique cover is.**
   `α(G) ≤ k` follows from covering `V(G)` by `k` cliques. `M` has `(w+1) + k`
   vertices; assign `s_i` of the `w+1` pad vertices to part `i` and make
   `{q_i} ∪ S_i` a clique. This needs `s_i ≤ λ_i` with `Σ s_i = w + 1`, i.e. it needs
   **`Σλ ≥ λ₁ + 1`** — which holds for **every** `λ` with `k ≥ 2`, because a second
   part is `≥ 1` (machine-checked: `28 598` partitions, `0` failures). **So the
   counting condition never blocks the assignment.** What is unproved is that a
   realization of `M` *containing those `k` cliques* exists; Observation C1-E says it
   sometimes does not, so the honest form of the attack is a **characterisation** of
   when it does, not a claim that it always does.
3. **One clean special case falls out and is proved here**: if `λ` is itself graphical
   then `G := K_{w+1} ⊔ H` realizes `M` with `α(G) = 1 + α(H) ≤ 1 + (k−1) = k`, since
   `H` has an edge. So **C1 holds for every graphical `λ`** — machine-confirmed on

   which is exactly why it is the obstruction.

### (g) **New, after a ledger grep — Lemma C1-F, the parity criterion**

Grepped first (`C1-D`, `C1-E`, `C1-F`, `Erdős–Gallai`, `graphical`, `step sequence`
across the draft, `problems/wowii`, `notes`, `orchestration`, `prompts`): **no prior
art**; the draft's only `Erdős–Gallai` uses are §7.14/§7.19's `[4,1,1,0,0,0]` witness.

> **Lemma C1-F.** For every partition `λ` with `λ₁ = w`, the list
> `M(λ) := [w]^{w+1} ∪ λ` is a step sequence **iff `Σλ` is even**.
>
> *Proof.* `Σ M = w(w+1) + Σλ ≡ Σλ (mod 2)` since `w(w+1)` is always even, and an even
> sum is necessary. For sufficiency, Erdős–Gallai at every `r`. `M` has
> `N = (w+1) + k` entries, of which at least `w+2` equal `w` (the `w+1` pad entries and
> `λ₁ = w`). **`r ≤ w`:** `Σ_{i≤r} d_i = rw`, and at least `w+2−r` further entries
> equal `w` survive past position `r`, each contributing `min(w,r) = r`, so the right
> side is at least `r(r−1) + (w+2−r)r = r(w+1) > rw`. **`r = w+1`:** left side
> `(w+1)w`; at least one entry of value `w` survives past position `w+1`, contributing
> `min(w, w+1) = w`, so the right side is at least `(w+1)w`. **`r ≥ w+2`:** every
> `d_i ≤ w ≤ r−2`, so the left side is at most `rw ≤ r(r−2) < r(r−1)`. ∎

Machine: over **all `28 628` partitions of `n = 1…30`**, "step sequence" and "`Σλ`
even" agree **`0` counterexamples**; the Erdős–Gallai predicate agrees with the process
on the same population, **`0` disagreements**; and the E–G inequality holds at **every**
`r` for **every** `λ` — `0` failures — so parity is the **only** obstruction, as the
proof claims.

**What it buys, stated exactly.** §7.25 (b)'s *"every partition of every `n ≤ 28` that
yields a terminating process — **10 268** cases"* was a **measured** figure. Lemma C1-F
makes it a **closed form**: the terminating cases are exactly the partitions of the
**even** `n`, and `Σ_{n = 2,4,…,28} p(n) = 10 268` — recomputed independently and
matched. It also subsumes the parity half of Theorem C1-2 (`w+c` even) and the abort
half of Lemma C1-A (`w` odd), which were proved separately and only for `k ≤ 2`.
**Status: PROVED (hand proof above), `0` S3 rounds** — the same standing as the family
it joins, and it is not counted as evidence for anything else.
