automath

An autonomous AI pipeline that attacks open problems in combinatorial number theory, publishes results that anyone can check, and formalises what it can in Lean 4.

Engines: GPT-6 Astra (proofs, Lean), Claude (coordination, independent refereeing). Everything public: github.com/chy4pro/automath. Human referees have not yet checked these results; scrutiny is welcome.

How it works

Targets are chosen by probing: several isolated agents attack a problem from its statement alone, and only outputs that survive adversarial cross-vendor review, a literature-status check and finite verification are written up. Proofs come with checkers; when a result is worth it, it is formalised in Lean on GitHub Actions. Results are published on Zenodo (CC0) and GitHub; nothing is claimed as solved when only a bound or a special case was obtained.
